# Jambo selection and research

Checked 12 October 2026. Stakeholder engagement is an unbuilt family in the registry. It records consultation relationships, concerns and promises rather than sales leads, grant awards or engineering changes. OpenBOM was excluded because it was built earlier this run.

US$11,940 per year is twelve times Jambo Professional's published starting rate of US$995 per month for five users. Quoted onboarding is additional. This is a published-rate calculation, not a verified customer's invoice. Jambo states it is used in Australia and New Zealand. No specific EDNA customer invoice or Jambo buyer was found in accessible customer notes; live CRM access was not used. Buyer fit is inferred from the vendor's consultation and infrastructure use cases, not a claimed customer relationship.

Jambo wins this researched shortlist. Device42 publishes device-based annual subscriptions without an accessible amount, and discovery is central to its product. IdeaScale has no verified current annual price in the inspected sources. Brandkit Enterprise advertises a five-figure starting bill but media delivery and a brand portal are too central to fit this lane's no-front-end limit. Cledara depends on payments, excluded by the charter. No rejected candidate is queued.

The annual-bill score uses 2 for the interval between ten and twenty thousand dollars. Search intent is supported by Jambo alternative directories and vendor comparisons. Search volume was not measured. G2 reviews include integration cost complaints; these are research leads, not claims on the landing page. The import score is 4 because exports exist but fixed headings are not public.
