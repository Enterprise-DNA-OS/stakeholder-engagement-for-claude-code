# Consultation record checks

Checked 12 October 2026. This is a scoped record-review aid for NZ consultation teams. It does not decide that consultation was sufficient, that consent exists, or that the organisation complies with privacy, resource management, Indigenous rights or Australian law. Those judgments need the applicable project rules and qualified review.

| Check | Source | What the command does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Missing collection purpose | NZ Privacy Act IPP1, https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/1/ | Flags a blank purpose for assessment. A written purpose alone does not establish lawful or necessary collection. |
| Retention review due or missing | NZ Privacy Act IPP9, https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/ | Prompts a review of whether the information is still needed. It never deletes a record. |
| Missing notice evidence | NZ Privacy Act IPP3A, https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/3a/ | Prompts assessment of collection notices, particularly for information collected indirectly. Exceptions and applicability require human review. |

IPP3A applies to indirect collection and took effect in May 2026. Assess the basis for indirect collection as well as the applicable notice requirements. This program stores an evidence reference or a reasoned exception record; it does not issue notices. Direct collection has separate obligations. The source page links the related principles.

Retention-review dates are chosen by the operator. They are not statutory deletion dates. The 30-day engagement gap and 14-day commitment window are local scheduling rules, not law. A missing notice evidence field is a review prompt, not a legal breach finding. Entering text does not prove compliance.

Issues cannot resolve while linked commitments remain open. Commitments need evidence before completion. These are operational checks, not statutory rules. The local history records actor and time, but an owner with database access can alter it; it is not a tamper-proof audit system.

Public database access is revoked and row security enabled. Trusted owner connections can still see all records. Shared use needs explicit roles, access policies, private attachment storage, retention procedures, tested backups and approved agent access. Do not expose the owner connection as a public endpoint. No outbound messages, legal notices or automatic deletion are implemented.
